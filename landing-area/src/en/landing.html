FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Helpful answers.

Everything you need to know before arriving with your campervan or motorhome.

How does the campervan area work off-season?

Off-season, Càmping La Mola operates strictly as a self-service campervan and motorhome area. It is 100% automated: you can complete check-in online on your smartphone on the day of arrival, from 9:00 to 22:00, and access pitches and facilities immediately without queuing at reception.

How close is the camping to Aigüestortes National Park and Espot Ski Resort?

The location is ideal: situated along road LV-5004 km 5, just 5 minutes (less than 2 km) from Espot village, where hikers access trails and the 4x4 taxi service to Saint Maurici Lake. Espot Esquí ski station is also less than 10 minutes away by vehicle.

What services are included in the overnight fee?

The fee covers vehicle parking, full access to indoor facilities with hot showers, drive-over grey water drainage, chemical toilet (black water) dumping station, and clean drinking water refill.

Do I need to book in advance?

No advance booking is required. You can simply complete the online check-in prior to departure or right when you pull up to the campsite entrance using your mobile phone.

Which vehicles can stay in the camper area?

All types of leisure vehicles are welcome, including campervans, motorhomes of any length, and cars towing caravans. Wide access tracks ensure straightforward manoeuvring.
